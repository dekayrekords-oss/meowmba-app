Meow Flow

Meow Flow has no avatar and no fixed layout. Meow notices unfinished intentions from what you say and do, like "the rear light is out" or "finish the heat fix". It follows them across apps, places and hours, and brings each one forward when it's useful. Work comes first. When a chance is about to pass, it interrupts: out loud, on the home screen, or as a notification if you're in another app. You decide how often.
