Meow's desk

A home-screen avatar for work days. Meow sits at a little desk, and each object in the room is one thing you track. Nothing is a widget or a card until you tap it.

Corkboard
Your project: Meowmba's Play review, tester days crossed off by hand
Monitor
Latest build and test run
Sticky note
What's next on your calendar
Letter tray
Texts, email and missed calls. Envelopes pile up when something needs you
Jar
Money: balances, bills, purchases and returns
Lamp
Lights up when Meow has an idea for you
Window
The weather, as the app already shows it
Tap an object on the phone: Meow walks to it and the details open in the app's own bottom sheet. Mic, camera and chat stay where they are today; there is no Care mode on work avatars. Project and build facts are real; messages and money are made-up examples.
