Work home screens

Three plain options. The avatar is the app's own eyes, kept small and calm. It says one useful sentence, and everything below it is ordinary text with one clear button per item. Tap anything. Mic, camera and chat stay put, and there's no Care mode. Meowmba facts are real; calls, bills and emails are examples.

A · Briefing

Reads like a note from an assistant. First what needs you, then your project, then the rest of the day. Everything fits on one screen without scrolling.

B · One thing at a time

Shows only the next thing that needs you, with the button to handle it. Handle it, skip it or snooze it, and the next one slides in. Built for busy days and people who don't want a list.

C · Your day

Your day as a simple timeline, with a green line marking now. Anything without a time, like bills and email, sits in chips at the top.
