Three work avatars

Each one is the whole home screen, built for a different kind of work day. Tap anything on the phones. Mic, camera and chat stay put, and there's no Care mode. Meowmba facts are real; customers, orders and money are made-up examples.

The Bench

Engineer

The avatar is an oscilloscope. At rest, its phosphor beam draws a face. Turn a knob and the same beam traces builds, open bugs or Play testers. Tap the screen for details. A paper tape at the bottom prints each event as it happens.

The Line

Project manager

The plan is drawn as a transit map. Each workstream is a coloured line that meets the others at interchange stations. A pulsing ring marks where you are now, and a red bar marks what's blocked. The split-flap board up top shows your next three deadlines.

The Counter

Business owner

The shop counter at close of day. Orders, appointments and returns hang on a ticket rail. The till prints today's sales on a receipt tape that grows as sales come in. The bell counts calls waiting, and unpaid invoices sit on the spike.
