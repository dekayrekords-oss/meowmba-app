Meow Assistant 2.0 / candidate 203 — ring Accessibility declaration draft
2026-10-03. Local preparation only; not saved or approved in Play Console.

Purpose: App functionality. This general assistant is NOT declared an accessibility tool.
Service: com.example.meowmba.core.ring.RingAccessibilityService
isAccessibilityTool: false.
The autonomous MeowAccessibilityService remains excluded from the public manifest.

Core feature:
A user pairs a compatible R08/QRing ring, agrees to the separate in-app disclosure,
enables Meowmba Ring Controls in Android, verifies physical ring input, chooses an
app allowlist and unpauses. Configured, deterministic gestures scroll, move the
click target, click its control, navigate, control media/volume, open a selected app
or camera, select a compatible photo shutter, or submit a configured Meow request.
The phone must be unlocked. The ring service performs deterministic configured
actions only from verified physical inputs. Ask Meow submits owner-configured text
through the normal typed-chat and task-approval pipeline; AI cannot synthesize ring
input or operate the public autonomous phone Accessibility service.
The optional scroll/click preset maps swipes to scrolling, single tap to click,
one tap plus swipe to horizontal target movement and two taps plus swipe to
vertical movement. Sleeping controls consume double tap as wake only.

Data accessed:
Ring key events; active foreground package; application-window bounds; control
structure, scrollable/enabled/clickable/visible state and photo-shutter labels.
The ring service does not record screen contents or send screen contents or raw
ring input to AI providers. Local gesture logs may be explicitly shared by the
owner with the selected diagnostic destination. Configured
Meow requests are stored privately on the phone; the owner-selected Ask Meow action
may send that request and normal conversation context to the selected AI provider.
Existing app-wide user-content/provider Data safety answers must cover those
requests; do not answer No for all app data based on local ring processing alone. Information is processed locally. Users may explicitly
share a connection/gesture diagnostic log through Android's share chooser.
Proposed access/control-data answer: local processing; configured Meow requests
use the existing app-wide user-content/provider declarations. Active-app/control
information is processed locally. The diagnostic export is a specific user-
initiated share action with an expected destination chosen in Android, rather
than automatic collection or sharing. This interpretation relies on Google's
on-device processing and user-initiated sharing exclusions; other app/SDK data
declarations remain unchanged. Recheck if diagnostic delivery changes.

Disclosure:
Shown automatically after a ring connection when consent is absent, and before
Open Accessibility settings. The disclosure explains access, purpose, local use,
allowed apps and withdrawal. Agree and continue is required; Not now/dismissal
does not consent. Consent withdrawal pauses, ends testing and cancels pending
ring touches, queued scrolls and pending configured requests. The expanded 203
purposes require the revised disclosure even if older ring consent was accepted. An already enabled Android service cannot act before app consent.

Evidence video still required:
Record opening the candidate app, pairing, the complete disclosure and affirmative

Host the short reviewer video at an accessible URL and complete the Play form.
