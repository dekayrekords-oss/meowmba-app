## Full description (2,871 / 4,000 characters)

Meet Meow Assistant: an animated AI companion for conversations, creative ideas and everyday tasks. Choose a supported AI provider or compatible on-device model, then connect the features you want.

TALK, TYPE AND REMEMBER
Ask questions by text or voice, keep notes and memories, and return to saved conversations. Voice features depend on microphone permission, the selected speech engine and any required model downloads or provider setup.

MAKE YOUR COMPANION YOUR OWN
Choose a character, adjust its appearance and size, and explore animated environments. New scenes include Saffron dunes, Alpine cableway, Coral twilight, Lantern courtyard and Crystal cavern. With a configured AI, create and review an avatar design, refine it, retry a failed example or cancel. Equip the design only when you approve it.

GET HELP WITH EVERYDAY TASKS
Open apps, prepare drafts and use supported tools. Connect a compatible Computer workspace for approved tasks. Save and preview supported small-file results, find verified copies in Files and retain older copies when a result changes. Computer features need separate setup; large transfers and a unified project workflow remain under development.

KEEP CONTROLS WITHIN REACH
Microphone and camera have their own controls. Fresh setup starts with the microphone off. Skipping sensor setup or denying access leaves both sensors off, including after restart. Driving mode offers a compact summary and scrollable details; alerts depend on permissions and regional sources. Configure it before driving. It is not a certified safety or emergency-response system.

CHOOSE YOUR AI SETUP
Use supported local models or connect a cloud provider. Local models require downloads and sufficient memory and storage. Cloud models, image generation and connected services may require an account, API key, internet connection or separate fees. A model appearing in a catalog does not guarantee every capability. Speed, quality and availability depend on your device, model and provider.

UNDERSTAND YOUR DATA
Local history is stored on your phone. Cloud models and connected services can receive the text, audio, images, files or relevant context needed for the feature you use. Review your settings, the app's privacy policy and the selected service's terms. Optional diagnostic sharing opens Android's share chooser so you decide where to send the report.

PLAY EDITION
App launching and drafting are supported. Autonomous Accessibility control of other apps' screens and learned screen-navigation replay are not included in this Play edition. Physical robot actuation is unavailable. Optional wearable and other hardware integrations depend on compatible equipment and remain subject to separate validation.

AI responses can be incomplete or incorrect. Review important information, generated content and proposed actions before relying on them.
