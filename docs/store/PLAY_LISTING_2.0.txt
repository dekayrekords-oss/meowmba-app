App name: Meow Assistant
Short description: AI chat, expressive avatars, memory, projects and smart-ring connection.

Make room for your ideas with Meow Assistant 2.0: an Android companion and workspace for conversations, projects, saved memories and connected Computer tasks. Choose a character, a supported AI model and the features that fit your day.

TURN AN IDEA INTO A NEXT STEP
Talk or type through a plan, prepare drafts and return to saved conversations. Keep projects, files and next steps together. Review generated content and proposed actions before using them.

YOUR COMPANION, YOUR SPACE
Explore expressive avatars, animated environments and the Desk's companion rooms. Adjust your character's appearance and size. With a configured generation provider, review and refine an avatar design before equipping it. Provider setup and separate charges may apply.

KEEP WORK IN CONTEXT
Use Flow to bring relevant work together, set North Stars and track next steps. Correct or pin saved facts and revisit saved work. Development Studio and Computer features connect supported workspaces and tools; review action requests and inspect returned results. Connections require separate setup, compatible endpoints and supported tools. Availability depends on your setup.

CONNECT A SMART RING
Scan for a compatible R08/QRing Bluetooth ring, connect or reconnect, and request on-demand battery and step readings. Explore supported heart rate, oxygen, temperature, HRV and stress sensor tests. Availability and results depend on the ring, firmware, fit and contact; wellness estimates are experimental. Readings stay on your phone. Ring setup includes connection status, disconnect controls and access to Android Bluetooth settings.

CHOOSE WHAT TO IMPORT
Select photos or videos through Android's picker, or select documents through the system file picker. Supported imports become local copies; your originals stay unchanged. Find saved results and supported previews in Files.

CHOOSE HOW MEOW THINKS
Connect supported cloud providers, a compatible custom API or a supported on-device model. Local models require downloads, compatible hardware and sufficient storage. Voice needs microphone permission and a supported engine or model. Vision needs a compatible model and explicitly granted camera or image access. Speed, quality and availability depend on your device and provider. Accounts, API keys, internet access and separate charges may apply.

KEEP YOUR CHOICES VISIBLE
Local history is stored on your phone. Microphone and camera have separate controls; fresh setup starts with the microphone off. Optional location context adds available location to the on-device context journal and conversations. Meow explains collection before Android asks; the Play edition uses approximate location while Meow is visible and stops receiving optional Flow location when you leave it. You can decline or revoke access. Cloud AI and connected services may receive relevant text, audio, images, files or context, including location context, needed for the feature you use. Review the privacy policy and provider terms.

The Play edition includes app launching, drafting and configured Computer tools. Ring features here are Bluetooth connection and on-demand readings; cross-app gesture control is not included. Hardware integrations require compatible equipment and separate setup.

Meow Assistant is in beta. AI can make mistakes; check important answers and proposed actions.
