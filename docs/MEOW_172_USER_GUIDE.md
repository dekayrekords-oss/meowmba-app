# Meow preview 172 — North Stars and actions

## North Stars

In **Flow**, tap **Adjust → North Stars**, or the North Star line below the date. Add a long-term goal, why it matters, its rough horizon, and the steps that make it possible. For example: “My band plays in Europe.” Suggested steps are editable. Check a step only after completing it.

Meow connects relevant local Flow items, recent context and notification excerpts to your goals. Open a goal to see why each connection was made, remove an incorrect connection, and read the weekly look back. Pin a goal to put it first and on the Desk corkboard. A connection means “this may matter”; it is not proof that you accomplished something.

## Scan a QR code

Say or type **“Scan a QR code”** or **“Open the camera and get this QR code.”** Allow camera access if Android asks. The rear-camera scanner opens visibly; point it at the code. Meow reports the decoded contents, or tells you if permission, the camera or scanning failed. Cancel returns without inventing a result. A decoded link is not opened automatically.

## Get help while using another app

1. In **Flow → Adjust**, find **Ask Meow** and add its Quick Settings tile. On older Android versions, edit Quick Settings and drag the tile into your active tiles.
2. Open the app you need help with, pull down Quick Settings and tap **Ask Meow**.
3. Approve Android's screen-sharing prompt. Choose the app/screen you want to share. Meow captures one picture and stops sharing.
4. Tap the **“Screen ready — ask Meow”** notification. Review the picture and extracted text, enter your question, and tap Ask.

You can also share a screenshot or selected text using Android's Share menu and choose Meow. Text extraction happens on the phone. Answers use an installed local chat model by default. If none is available, you can explicitly choose **Use my configured AI** for the question; that can send the extracted text and your question to your configured provider. Images are not uploaded by this screen-help flow. Protected apps can prevent screenshots. Captures expire after ten minutes, and consumed capture files are removed.

Screen help gives guidance from the shared picture. It does not keep watching or tap buttons for you. Share a new picture after the screen changes.

## Open a message

Tap a message's action in Flow or Meow's notification. Meow uses the messaging app's original notification action, which carries its conversation destination. When that action points at Jim's conversation, Jim's conversation opens. Meow cannot override a messaging app that provides only a generic inbox action. If the original action has expired, Meow says so instead of silently opening an unrelated inbox. Opening a conversation does not count as sending a reply.

## Updating

Install this preview over the existing preview. It retains the package identity and signing certificate, so Android preserves your settings, keys and app data. Do not uninstall first. The separate Play edition remains a separate installation. The encrypted backup controls remain available in Settings.
