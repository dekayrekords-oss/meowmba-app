## Behavior

- Missing or invalid idle preferences resolve to Flow. Existing explicit Lumen,
  Orb and Off choices remain valid.
- Flow stays visible. After the selected inactivity interval, Desk visits Flow
  through `openFlow()`, preserving the saved home and Back navigation.
- Chat, menus, drawers, settings, browser and paused screens block the timer.
  Explicit avatar homes and driving mode do not participate in idle home navigation.
- Optional avatars still show incoming Flow ribbons and their click/swipe actions.
  Any new touch resets the inactivity countdown; avatar actions finish before
  their overlay is removed.
- Flow → Adjust → When idle exposes Flow, Lumen, Orb and Off. Off keeps the page.

The timer uses cancellable coroutine delays tied to interactions instead of
wall-clock deadlines, so clock corrections cannot prematurely switch the view.
