4. **Our First Working AIOS Milestone** — demonstrate an agreed acceptance check and the evidence it passes.
5. **What Broke During AIOS Setup—and How We Investigated** — use a real incident, relevant logs, the changed configuration or code, and a retest. Publish only when such an incident exists.
6. **Connecting AIOS to Our Tools** — show one implemented integration if it becomes part of the design. Do not assume it runs on Meow or controls existing apps.
7. **Running and Maintaining AIOS** — cover actual operational procedures once there is a working system to manage.
8. **What We Learned Bringing Up AIOS** — review verified milestones, remaining limitations, and the next chosen step.

These titles describe a potential story, not a promise of features, dates, or completed work. Interleave milestones with app ideas, other builds, and Meow server videos instead of delaying the whole channel for AIOS.
