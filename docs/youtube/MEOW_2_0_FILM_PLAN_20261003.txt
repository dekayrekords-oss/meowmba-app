It jumps, investigates, organizes and tinkers; match cuts connect these actions to the actual app. Transformations replace the whole body with the raccoon, bunny, dragon or other form. Preserve the familiar identity across forms.
The same fictional adult with dark wavy hair and a forest-teal sweater appears throughout. Ring close-ups recur at the camera, car and Desk, with the main ring section at 03:50.
Driving/context, Desk and the ring-control section have 35 seconds each: 105 seconds of the five-minute plan. Here hands-free means controlling the phone with the ring without touching the phone: swipe to scroll, aim the click target with configured tap/swipe combinations, then tap to click. Voice is an additional feature. Keep gestures, the resulting phone action and the cat's matching performance in the same shot or a clear match cut. Setup, ring readings and voice are distinct capabilities.
Narrator: original professional British baritone with warm theatrical delivery, or an authorized Matt Berry recording. No synthesized imitation. User and Orb dialogue use original voices.
