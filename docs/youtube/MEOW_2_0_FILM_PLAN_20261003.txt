CREATIVE THREAD
The approved charcoal cat with lime eyes, glowing green circuit whiskers, mint collar and power tag guides the film. It jumps, investigates, organizes and tinkers; match cuts connect these actions to the actual app. Transformations replace the whole body with the raccoon, bunny, dragon or other form. Preserve the familiar identity across forms.
The same fictional adult with dark wavy hair and a forest-teal sweater appears throughout. Ring close-ups recur at the camera, car and Desk, with the main ring section at 03:50.
Driving, Desk voice use and the ring/hands-free section have 35 seconds each: 105 seconds of the five-minute plan. Keep the phone mounted during voice demonstrations. Setup and on-demand reading requests remain visibly separate from voice interaction.
Narrator: original professional British baritone with warm theatrical delivery, or an authorized Matt Berry recording. No synthesized imitation. User and Orb dialogue use original voices.

09 — RING + HANDS-FREE | 03:50–04:25
Picture: foreground ring macro, then actual Smart Ring setup/connection status. Pair a compatible Bluetooth ring and request on-demand battery and step readings. Show the actual returned data on the phone. The physical ring is represented without an invented display. Use sequential inserts of the same device, not a requirement for two phones. Then return to voice at the Desk, hands occupied, and a mounted car voice cutaway. Pairing/readings and voice conversation are distinct capabilities; there is no causal ring-gesture-to-app cut in this public release film.
Suggested split: 0–6 seconds ring and connection; 6–16 actual on-demand readings; 16–25 Desk voice; 25–32 mounted car voice; 32–35 return to Flow and the cat.
Narration: “Connect your compatible Bluetooth ring and request battery or step readings when you need them. See the connection and the returned information on your phone. Then keep Meow in place and continue by voice—at your Desk or in the car—with your hands staying with the task.”
Supporting montage: talk/type, file import, memory search/correction/pin, calendar or alarms, weather or directions. Keep real UI readable; do not rush every tool into a single shot.
Play scope: ring connection and on-demand readings are supported. Accessibility scrolling, clicking and cursor gestures belong to the private phone edition and are excluded from this default Play cut. They may be shown only in a separate clearly labeled private-phone treatment if requested. No ring control of a car or desktop is claimed. Wellness sensors, if added, require the experimental/nonmedical label and real hardware results.
