CURRENT FEATURE BOUNDARIES
- Camera recognition replaces QR scanning in clip 2. No QR segment remains.
- Flow is the everyday interface demonstrated while out and about. Desk is the interface selected for focused desk work.
- DriveAvatarPolicy automatically shows the full avatar when a drive is detected, and restores Flow/Desk when the drive ends. Show driving setup and permissions first; detection is not guaranteed at the instant someone enters a car.
- Idle defaults to Flow after inactivity in the next build. Lumen/Orb are explicit settings options; Off retains the current page. Existing explicit choices remain. Idle Desk visits Flow without changing the saved home.
- Wireless-charger avatar behavior exists as an opt-in configuration; Android detects wireless power, not a named magnetic dock. Keep this separate from the default idle view. Show a configured charger only after a real device test.
- TV scene: explicitly select Orb, then use a compatible phone/TV screen-mirroring mechanism. No app-specific Chromecast receiver was found in the source; do not fabricate a Cast button. Live mirroring, microphone input, reply audio and echo handling require device acceptance.
- Physical robot control is disabled in this build: RobotBaseControlPolicy.CONTROL_AVAILABLE=false. Moving around the desk belongs in a clearly labeled future-concept segment, not a released-feature demonstration. No app-controlled robot movement or motor test is authorized by this video task.
- Computer assistance requires a configured phone/computer workspace. Robot movement and computer assistance are separate capabilities.
- The public Play build offers Bluetooth ring connection and on-demand readings. Cross-app ring gestures belong to the private phone edition and are excluded. Wellness readings are experimental, hardware-dependent, and nonmedical.
- Custom avatar creation uses the selected AI configuration to create/save an animated native form; film art must be matched to the actual generated in-app result.
- All 16 built-in appearances are introduced. Custom forms have no exhaustive fixed inventory.
- Higgsfield currently reports 50 credits and a free plan. Earlier image and video submissions were rejected for plan entitlement. No new generation job was submitted during this update.
- Google Play release was last verified in review. Confirm live availability before choosing an “available now” ending.
