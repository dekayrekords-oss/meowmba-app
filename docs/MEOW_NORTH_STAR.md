# Meow's 4th dimension — guiding the user toward the life they're building (design, 2026-09-24)

## The owner's words

> The app should live in the "4th dimension". Humans are only aware of 15% of what's going on around
> them; this should give the user that 4th dimension to help them navigate the life they are trying to
> live. It should be aware of the other 80%. If we are all trying to accomplish what the 4th dimension
> has set us out to do, that means it already exists — we just need to be guided to achieve it.
> For example, I want my band to play in Europe. It has not been decided, but every action I take
> determines how real that possibility is. The app needs to form these connections and push the user
> to their ultimate goals and achievements.

This sits above everything built so far. Flow, the context journal, notifications, places and Laya are
Meow's senses. The **North Star layer** is what they are for.

## What Meow keeps

**North Stars** — the user's big aims, in their own words ("My band plays in Europe"). Captured when the
user says them ("I want…", "my dream is…", "one day I'll…") or adds one in Flow. Each holds:

- **Why it matters** (what the user said about it), and a rough horizon (this year / someday).
- **What makes it more real** — the ingredients Meow learns from the goal. For "band in Europe":
  a live set that's tight, recordings and a press kit, an audience outside the home city, contacts
  (European bookers, festivals, promoters, bands that tour there), money for travel, passports,
  time off, a manager or agent.
- **Momentum** — a simple, honest measure of how much has moved lately: things done, contacts made,
  opportunities taken or missed. Shown as "moving / quiet for 3 weeks", never a fake percentage.

## How Meow forms the connections (the 80%)

Everything Meow already notices is checked against each North Star.

| What Meow sees | The connection it makes |
|---|---|
| A message from someone who books shows, a promoter, another touring band | "Mara books the Berlin club circuit — reply today; that's a Europe door." |
| Calendar: a gig, rehearsal, recording session | Counts toward "tight set" / "recordings"; suggests the next step after it. |
| Your own words in chat or voice ("we sold out the Echo") | Logged as progress; asks if there's a recording or photo for the press kit. |
| Money texts and bills | Notices a travel fund growing or shrinking. |
| Places and time: you're near the rehearsal space, you have a free evening | "Two hours free — the EPK draft is 80% done." |
| Things you share with + (flyers, emails, contracts) | Reads dates, venues, contacts; links them to the goal. |
| Quiet weeks | "Nothing toward Europe in 3 weeks. One small thing: email one festival." |

Laya asks the typed questions ("Does this message move North Star X forward?", "How much?"); rules
catch the obvious cases when Laya is unavailable or the phone is warm.

## How Meow pushes (proactive, not chatty)

- **One next step at a time**, at the right moment: small, concrete and doable now ("Send the live video
  to 2 festivals — 10 min"), shown in Flow with the North Star it serves.
- **Doors, not chores**: when something opens a path (a contact, an invite, a deadline for festival
  applications), it's raised quickly, with why it matters for the goal.
- **Weekly look back**: what moved, what was missed, one focus for the week. Short, on screen.
- **Never** acts for the user (no messages sent, no money spent) without a yes.

## Build steps

1. **North Star store + capture** (Room table, "I want / my dream is" statements, Flow › Adjust list).
2. **Ingredients**: Laya/rules break a goal into what makes it real; the user can edit.
3. **Linking**: each new Flow item, message, calendar event, journal line and attachment is scored
   against the North Stars; links are stored and shown ("→ Europe").
4. **Next step + momentum** in Flow; weekly look back.
5. **Desk**: the corkboard pins the main North Star and its next step.

Principles still apply: on the phone only; heat budget (linking runs on events and in batches, not
continuously); the user can see and delete everything Meow links.
