# Meowmba Cat (mascot)

**Role:** mascot / hero of comics and videos
**First appearance:** Meowmba #1 — "The Idea" (demo comic)
**Owner:** Meowmba studio

## Appearance (LOCKED — follow exactly)

- Species/breed: cartoon cat, chibi-round, big head
- Body color: `#F59E2D` (orange tabby)
- Markings: three head stripes `#D97F14` (one center, two sides)
- Eyes: big round dark `#222` with white shine dot top-left
- Nose: pink `#E0566B`, small triangle-ish
- Ears/tail: pointed ears same body color; thick fluffy tail, darker tip allowed
- Size/proportions: head ≈ 45% of total height; stubby legs
- Outfit/accessories: none (default)
- Distinguishing marks: the 3-stripe forehead + huge eyes

## Personality

- 3–5 traits: confident, scheming-but-sweet, dramatic, secretly soft
- Voice: short punchy lines, one-liners, occasional meow punctuation
- Catchphrases: "MEOWMBA!", "The humans will never believe a cat made it."

## Consistency checklist (what must NEVER change)

1. Orange `#F59E2D` with exactly 3 forehead stripes
2. Big round eyes with white shine dot
3. Chibi proportions (huge head)
