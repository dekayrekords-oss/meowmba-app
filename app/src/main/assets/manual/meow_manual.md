# The Meow manual

## Meow's place

Meow lives in three rooms. The office is in the middle, the living room is to the left and the bedroom is to the right. On a large or unfolded screen you see all three. On the folded cover screen you see one room at a time, and the view follows Meow as it wanders. Tap the room names at the top to look next door; Meow comes along.

Tap Meow to pet it. Long-press Meow for **Meow's routine**: how often it visits other rooms while you're away (Never, Rarely, Sometimes or Often) and the **room size** (60–200%; bigger zooms in and the view follows Meow, with buttons to step between rooms). Both are also in Flow → Adjust → When idle. Turned sideways, the menu moves to the top and the rooms fill the screen edge to edge.

Meow has habits of its own: it looks things up on the computer (an animation only; nothing is searched or sent), reads on the couch, plays the old console, chases a toy mouse, gets the zoomies, bats a ball of yarn and the toy on the cat tower, knocks the pen off the desk, sits on the keyboard, chases its tail, hides in the cardboard box, rolls over for a belly rub, kneads the bed before a nap, naps in the sunbeam, watches birds on the windowsill, and at night swats at the moth by the lamp.

The windows show the real time of day and your weather: sunrise, midday, golden hour, sunset and night, with drifting clouds, rain or snow. Keep an eye out for the occasional plane (and something rarer). The office cursor blinks, the sticky note lifts in a draught, and the old TV plays a game. Left alone, Meow breathes, twitches an ear, flicks its tail, looks around, then sits down and eventually lies down. Tap the bed, the food tray, the litter box, the couch or a cat tower and Meow goes there. These are only for fun: there are no hunger or care meters.

## The office

- **Computer:** the monitor on the old Gateway 2000. It lists your Development Studio projects and what last happened in each. Open Computer or Development Studio from it.
- **Corkboard:** your pinned North Star or project.
- **Sticky note:** what's next.
- **Letter tray:** people waiting on you.
- **Jar:** money things coming up.
- **Lamp:** on at night. By day it glows, with a little spark, when Meow is keeping an idea for you.
- **Clock:** opens your phone's alarms and timers.
- **Drawers:** folders for your favourite apps and tools.
- **Ask Meow:** type a question without leaving the room.

## The living room

- **Stereo:** the song that's playing, with play, pause and next. Its display shows whether it's playing, the app, the song and the artist.
- **Big-screen TV:** what's on YouTube.
- **Bookshelf:** the audiobook you're listening to, your audiobook app, and this manual.
- **Picture:** tap it to put one of your photos in the frame, or to bring back the painting. Meow keeps a small copy on this phone only; your original isn't changed.
- **Cat tower:** behind the couch. Meow climbs it in two jumps.

Turn the phone sideways and the living room and bedroom get more space: a wider bookshelf, a much bigger TV and a longer bed with a nightstand.

The stereo, TV and bookshelf need Android's notification access for Meow, which lets Meow see media titles. Meow reads them only while the room is on screen. It never listens through the microphone for them.

## The bedroom

- **Big window:** the weather, with clouds over the city. The moon is in the office window.
- **Old TV and console:** opens the MAME arcade (MAME4droid). You need MAME4droid and your own game files.

## When you drive

When Meow detects a drive, the full avatar takes over the screen. When the drive ends, you're back in Flow or the Desk.

## When idle

Flow is the default idle view. After the selected time without a touch, the Desk opens Flow without changing your saved home; Back returns to the Desk. In **Flow → Adjust → When idle**, choose **Lumen** or **Orb** if you want an idle avatar instead, or **Off** to keep the current page. These optional avatars show new Flow items as a ribbon; tap to return to the page. Existing explicit idle choices are retained.

## North Stars

In Flow, tap **Adjust → North Stars**, or tap the North Star line below the date. Add a long-term goal, why it matters, and the steps that make it possible. Meow connects relevant Flow items and recent context to your goals. A connection means "this may matter"; it is not proof that you accomplished something.

## Location context

Location context for the journal and conversations is optional. Meow explains how location is used before asking Android for access. The Google Play edition uses location only while Meow is visible and stops receiving location when you leave it. Choose **Only while using the app**, or decline. The private phone edition can also use background location if you separately choose **Open Android settings → Permissions → Location → Allow all the time**. You can turn access off at any time. Location history stays in the phone's journal; selected cloud AI may receive relevant location context when answering your requests.

Smart Ring supports optional user-operated controls in the Google Play edition. In Settings → Smart Ring, review the disclosure, enable Meowmba Ring Controls in Android Accessibility, connect your R08 and verify each physical gesture in the tester. Choose allowed foreground apps, turn the tester off, then resume controls. The ring service does not record screen contents or send screen contents or raw ring input to AI; local connection/gesture diagnostics are shared only when you choose a destination. The autonomous phone-control Accessibility service remains excluded from Play.

Choose **How the ring works**: Media control (track swipes, play/pause, stop and tap + swipe volume), Scrolling and navigation (swipes scroll, single tap clicks the target, tap + swipe moves left/right, two taps + swipe moves down/up, double tap goes Back), Photo taking (Next opens a camera, single tap clicks a compatible photo shutter, double tap goes Back), or Custom commands. Custom mode can map gestures to navigation, media, camera, a chosen installed app, or a saved Meow request. Saved requests are local settings and run like typed chat through your selected provider and normal task approvals; avoid saving secrets. Changing modes or bindings pauses controls and preserves custom bindings for later. Opening a camera does not automatically add it to allowed apps. Photo mode requires an accessible photo-only shutter label; unsupported cameras report that the shutter is unavailable.

Scrolling first uses the active scrollable control, preferring the area under the target, then a focused or main pane. If the control rejects scrolling, Meow tries a swipe inside that area. A short bounded queue preserves rapid swipes and clears when controls or the foreground app change. Pausing stops Meow remapping; the ring's native Android media keys may still operate outside Meow. Sensor readings stay on your phone and are not medical measurements.

## Scan a QR code

Say or type **"Scan a QR code"**. The rear camera opens visibly. Meow reports what the code contains, and never opens a decoded link on its own.

## Get help in another app

Add the **Ask Meow** Quick Settings tile. In any app, tap it, share the screen once, then ask your question. Text is read on the phone; answers use a local model unless you choose your configured AI.

## Messages

Tapping a message's action opens that conversation through the messaging app's own notification action. Opening a conversation never sends anything.

## Privacy

Meow's saved memories and Flow records are stored on your phone. When you choose a cloud AI, your requests and relevant context go to that provider. Speech, images and other tools can use their own selected providers. Connected SDKs may handle device identifiers, diagnostics and usage information. Check the privacy policy for details. Meow never sends a message, buys something or publishes a post on your behalf without your approval.

## Projects and files

Use **Projects** to keep a longer piece of work together, and **Memory** to find, correct or pin facts you want Meow to retain. **Development Studio** keeps a working session while you move between screens. The desk's computer opens your projects and Computer tools; connecting a computer or setting up the phone workspace is optional.

Open **Files** to browse saved results. Tap **+ → Import photos or videos** to choose media through Android's picker, or **+ → Import files** for documents and other files. Only the files you choose are copied into Meow's library. **Connect phone folder** grants access to the folder you select for read-only browsing; import a file to organize its copy with AI.

In the Google Play edition, **Choose phone files** uses these pickers instead of scanning your whole media library. **Clean up library** proposes duplicate, empty or large files for review. Nothing is selected automatically. Moving a library copy to Trash can be undone; permanent deletion requires confirmation. Originals on your phone are unchanged.

## Facebook posts

Say “Make a Facebook post for my page about …”. Meow opens Facebook and asks
which Page/profile to use from the destinations visible in its account switcher.
Choose a destination, or use **More pages** to scroll its list. **Cancel** stops
without posting. This assisted in-app Facebook workflow requires the private preview edition, a signed-in Facebook app and Meow Accessibility enabled. The Google Play edition does not include Accessibility control: create a draft with Meow, open Facebook, choose your destination and paste the draft yourself.

Meow prepares the draft in Facebook. Review the account, audience and text before
posting. Preparing a draft does not publish it. If Facebook does not expose the
page list or Meow cannot verify the selected account, it asks you to continue
manually. A browser preview is never proof that a Facebook post was published.

## Website previews

Completed previews stay in place. Meow stops after completion, a failed model
request or its action limit. Ask for another change to continue.


## Saved memories

Open **Memory** to search what Meow has saved, including older records. Choose **Correct** to replace a saved fact; the original chat stays unchanged. Choose **Pin** to keep a memory during automatic cleanup, or **Unpin** to return it to your normal retention setting. You can still delete a pinned memory. Pins do not make every answer repeat that memory.

Correction dates and source message IDs help you inspect a record. Export makes a JSON copy with correction and pin information. Use **Backup & updates** for an encrypted backup before changing devices. Deleting a saved memory does not erase the original conversation or older backup files.

Recall remains local to the app's memory store and uses the model you selected for your conversation. This update does not add server memory synchronization or download another model.
